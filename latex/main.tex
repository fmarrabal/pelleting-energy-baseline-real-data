\title{Explainable production-adjusted electrical-energy baselines for industrial feed pelleting}

\input{sections/authors}

\begin{abstract}
Electricity use in industrial feed pelleting varies with the quantity produced, the product, the pelleting line and the batch organization, so neither raw consumption nor a single specific energy consumption (SEC) figure says much about energy performance. This retrospective study developed and chronologically evaluated an explainable, production-adjusted electricity baseline and asked whether histories of completed operations improve it. The data are 2,745 records from three pelleting lines, 2,742 with positive recorded energy; the inputs are dosed mass, dosing-batch count, product characteristics, pelleting-line identity and calendar variables. Seven methods were compared on 800 chronologically ordered development targets; the selected Ridge regression was then frozen and evaluated against line-specific SEC on 798 later records. Eight current-input, history-enabled, temporal and pretrained configurations were also compared on 584 matched targets with 16 completed same-line operations as context. Ridge reduced test MAE from 61.29 to 52.45~kWh, 14.42\% below line-specific SEC; this is lower prediction error, not an energy saving. On the matched population the Temporal Fusion Transformer reached 53.65~kWh against 53.55~kWh for current-input Ridge, with no evidence of paired improvement, and the tested N-HiTS, TimesFM~2.5 and Chronos-2 configurations were less accurate. Performance was most consistent on Pellet~2 and Pellet~3; Pellet~1 also improved over SEC but remained systematically underestimated. Fixed 90\% and 95\% residual intervals covered 85.96\% and 93.23\% of the test records. Ridge provides a transparent retrospective baseline; prospective use requires verified inputs and measurement boundaries, line-specific acceptance criteria and validation on a new consecutive period.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
\item Production-adjusted electricity baseline from 2,745 real feed-pelleting records
\item Frozen Ridge model cuts test MAE by 14.42\% relative to line-specific SEC
\item Completed same-line histories, TFT, N-HiTS, TimesFM 2.5 and Chronos-2 add no accuracy
\item Exact additive decomposition traces each kWh estimate to recorded production inputs
\item Fixed 90\%/95\% residual intervals cover 85.96\% and 93.23\% of later test records
\end{highlights}
